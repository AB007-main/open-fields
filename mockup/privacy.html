Privacy policy

This is placeholder text for the mockup. A real policy has to be written or reviewed by someone qualified before launch, particularly because this site will have users under 13.

What we collect

  • If you make an account: your email, display name, and profile photo if you upload one.
  • If you submit or confirm a field: what you submitted, and roughly when.
  • Basic analytics: pages viewed, no cross-site tracking, no ad profiling.
  • Your location, only if you tap "use my location", and only to sort fields by distance. It is not stored.

What we don't do

  • Sell your data.
  • Serve personalized ads to anyone under 13.
  • Track you across other sites.

Your account

You can edit or delete your account at any time from your profile. Deleting removes your profile and comments. Field confirmations stay, anonymized, because removing them would silently make the map wrong.
